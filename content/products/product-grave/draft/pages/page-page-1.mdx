## Create your first page

Open the editor and choose **New page**. Give your page a title and a short description.

<Steps>
<Step title="Make it yours">
Write a helpful introduction. Use the slash menu to add a callout, a code example, or a card.
</Step>
<Step title="Find the right place">
Organize the page in your product navigation so readers can find it.
</Step>
<Step title="Share your work">
Review your changes and publish your product.
</Step>
</Steps>
